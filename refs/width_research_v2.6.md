# Front guard width / length references (for v2.6)

| Guard | Length | Width | How it passes the fork legs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mudhugger EVO Long (zip-tie) | 525 (front 185 / rear 340) | 90 (markup image) | Body sits under the arch above the leg tops; downward keel + leg straps; near-constant width |
| Mudhugger FRX (Fox 36 photos) | 435 | 90 | Tucks under arch, ties round arch top + legs; sides only wrap a modest part of the tyre |
| Cube Acid Mud Shield Long | 405 | 80 (under arch) - 125 (front body) | Narrow 80 under the arch, side wings with rubber pads press on the lowers' inner faces, flares to 125 ahead of the fork |
| Sico Amflow PL/PX/PR (scanned) | 440 | not published | Waisted saddle at the arch (narrow under the brace, flares fore/aft); ties through the waist |
| RRP ProGuard Mini/Std/Max | 386 / 494 / 582 | not published | Brace indent (+4 mm tyre clearance), "seal shield" sides wider and lower near the seals; 6 ties (2 arch, 2 per leg) |
| Fox 36 MY26 Mud Guard (short) | ~190 | ~115 | Bolts to mounts on the INSIDE of the 2026 36 lowers - sits between the legs where Fox designed room for it |
| Fox XL (2021-25 36/38) | std +3" front, +2.5" rear | not published | Bolts to arch back + bleed-port mounts; sits between/behind the lowers |
| MarshGuard Original | 220 | 265 flat blank (flexible PE) | Flexible sheet bent over the tyre, 4 ties; conforms to whatever gap there is |
| Printables sjc115 2026 Fox 36 fender | n/a | 100 | Built round 59.9 mm radius under the crown (2026 36 chassis) |

Takeaways: zip-tie guards that pass between the lowers stay ~80-90 wide there (EVO/FRX 90, Cube 80 at the arch).
Only guards bolted to purpose-made mounts (Fox, 100-115) are wider, on a chassis with more leg gap.
Ones that need extra width flare ahead of the fork (Cube 125) - the look James does not want.
AWL HD measured gap 85 at 40 mm below the brace -> 82 max (1.5 mm each side), consistent with Cube 80 / Mudhugger 90 class.

theoddspoke.com.au (Fox MY26 short 115x190); thelostco.com (Fox XL); sportokay.com (MarshGuard 265x220).
