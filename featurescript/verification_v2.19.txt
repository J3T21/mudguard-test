v2.19 verification (offline, no Onshape)

What changed from v2.18
  The collar blister loft, the matching cut, and the R10 tyre-side blend are not built.
  The outer R4 join fillet is not applied, so nothing is added outside the wall plane.
  The leg sheet is one surface. It leaves the guard side wall in the same plane (|y| = 48.5),
  bows away from the leg, and returns to that plane. Thickness is 2.5 mm measured normal
  to the face. The only wing holes are the D125 pair. Brace ties, the stepped joint,
  the R32 ends and the 250 mm bed fit are unchanged.

The bow
  Starts at the wall edge, D = 31.99 mm below the brace, tangent to the vertical plane.
  Depth 2.8 mm over 10 mm, then back to the plane over the next 22 mm. Both ends are
  quintic smoothsteps, so slope and curvature are zero there.
  Belly |y| = 45.7 at D = 42.0. Against the OD 55 collar (surface at |y| = 45.0) that is
  0.7 mm of interference. Against the local taper at that station the belly is 0.87 mm clear.
  The flush root line itself is still 2.64 mm into the upper leg. That zone is only D 32.0
  to 36.8 (4.7 mm of height); below that the face is within 1 mm, and from D = 44 it is clear
  of the taper. The root has to stay on the wall plane, so that short tangent is the
  interference plastic flex still covers.
  Back on the plane by D = 64. The D125 holes are in the flat sheet.
  OD 45 leg (D >= 90): the flat face at |y| = 48.5 is 1.50 mm clear.
  Stanchion OD 36: 6.0 mm clear at the flat face, more at the belly. No intersection.
  Max tyre (69.2): the bowed inner face is 9.15 mm clear at its closest sample
  (D = 47, inner |y| = 43.45). The 8 mm floor holds. Assegai is further away.

What the test reports, and what fails
  One string, also the throw message:
    v2.19 volA volB tyreGap stanchion collar od45 legMin holes bedB rootY bowY
  collar is the belly's intrusion into the OD 55 radius (0.70in). od45 is the flat
  sheet's clearance to the OD 45 leg (1.50clr). legMin is the worst signed gap of the
  face to the design leg, negative where it still intersects (the root tangent).
  None of those three fail the test.
  It does fail if the tyre gap is under 8 mm, the sheet meets the stanchion, the root
  is not on the wall plane, the belly is missing, the sheet does not return to the plane,
  anything sticks past |y| = 48.5, the hole count is wrong, the bed exceeds 250 mm,
  or a volume is outside ±8% (A 57.5–67.5 around 62.5, B 114–134 around 124).
