v2.17.1 offline verification (no Onshape)

Why v2.17 measured 1.1 mm
  Offline modelled the leg-facing face at |y|=43.5.
  Clearance to the 55 mm collar (radius 27.5, centre |y|=72.5) is 72.5 - 43.5 - 27.5 = 1.50 mm.
  The shell loft was built 0.4 mm proud of that face (the "skin" that was supposed to be cut off).
  The cut started on the same plane as the intended face, so the 0.4 mm skin remained.
  Real face |y|=43.9. Gap 72.5 - 43.9 - 27.5 = 1.10 mm. That is the Onshape result legclr 1.1.
  It is not the rib (the rib is inboard of the shell) and not an R4 remnant (those sit near |y|=46-48.5
  and would have been a clash, not a 1.1 mm gap).

v2.17.1 face
  Spec cylinder unchanged: 1.5 mm off OD 55, |y|=43.5.
  Extra inset 0.5 mm, so the loft outer is |y|=43.0.
  The cut then bites 0.3 mm into that loft, so the kept face is |y|=42.7.
  No proud skin. Wall thickness after the bite is still 2.5 mm (the shell is built 2.8 mm and 0.3 mm is cut off).
  Flat from D 16.02 to 38.02 (22 mm). Bottom ramp 64 mm, D 38.02 to 102.02, back to |y|=48.5.
  64 mm (was 51) keeps the ramp from returning to the sheet before the leg has tapered.

Clearance to the design leg (collar OD 55 to D=20, then straight to OD 45 at D=90), centreline
  Flat, kept face |y|=42.7: minimum 2.30 mm at D=16.0.
  Ramp while the face is still inboard of |y|=48.2: minimum 1.81 mm.
  Last 3 mm of the ramp, and the original lower sheet, sit at the designed 1.50 mm gap to OD 45.
  So the blister that Onshape measured at 1.1 mm is now 2.30 mm.
  If a 0.4 mm skin were left again the flat would still be 1.90 mm, above the 1.3 mm test.
  Whole-body minimum is the unchanged lower-sheet gap, 1.50 mm, which passes >= 1.3.

Stanchion OD 36 and the tyre are unchanged in direction: the face moved inboard, away from the stanchion,
and the rib is still on the tyre side of the skin. v2.17 samples were stanchion 6.0 mm, max tyre 9.16 mm,
Assegai 10.79 mm. Moving the face from 43.5 to 42.7 adds 0.8 mm to the tyre side and does not close those gaps.

Everything else is v2.17: no window, only the D=125 wing-tie pair, brace ties at s +/-12.5, R32 ends,
no lip chamfer, R4 join fillets, stepped joint. Bed fit is unchanged (the face moved inward).
