v2.19.3.1 verification (offline). Base: v2.19.3 (tag v2.19.3, 5b3ec19), which failed its one Onshape test with
"@opBoolean: BOOLEAN_BAD_INPUT" at the guard + boss "merge" union (line 825), before clipY and the joint.

Two likely causes, both fixed:
  (a) guardSolid returned a transient query (qUnion(evaluateQuery(...))). bossCut splits the boss into four lumps,
      after which that query can resolve to none or only one of them, so the union got bad or too few tools.
      Now guardSolid returns qCreatedBy(id, BODY)->qBodyType(SOLID), and merge takes every solid present except the tie
      slot cutters (guard + all boss lumps + rib if on). It throws a plain message if fewer than 2 bodies go in or more
      than 1 comes out.
  (b) shared closing line between differently lofted bodies (the boss / guard bottom face).

Audit rule applied: a body lofted with the new tangent end conditions is assumed NOT to share faces exactly with any body
made another way; where two bodies were meant to share a closing line / face / tangency, one is moved >= 0.5 mm.
