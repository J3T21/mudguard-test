v2.19.2 verification (offline, no Onshape)

Why v2.19.1 failed
  Onshape threw BOOLEAN_INVALID at the v2.14 deep-patch union (id + "patchU") before any check.
  The leg sheet was already unioned to the guard. Its embed was 0.05 mm proud of |y|=48.5
  and stepped back to the wall plane. The deep loft's outer face is the guard outer profile
  inset by 0.05 mm, so the two faces sat about 0.1 mm apart.

Order now
  The guard is merged, patched, trimmed and clipped with no leg sheet on it.
  The sheets are unioned after clipY. Embed and lower flat are 0.5 mm proud of |y|=48.5.
  wingShave then cuts y past 48.5 over the whole sheet, a 0.5 mm skin, and throws if it
  removes under 20 mm3. Expected removal is about 4000 mm3 (6 mm embed plus the 66 mm lower
  flat, both sides, 0.5 mm thick, 56 mm wide).

Corner rounds
  The bite sketch used plane normal +Y and xDir +X, so sketch y was world -Z and the tools
  were built at z = -zTip, clear of the sheet. The plane x-direction is now -X and the sketch
  x is negated, so sketch y is +world Z. Each side must lose at least 10 mm3 or the feature
  throws. A square-minus-quarter-circle of R8 through the 3.0 mm pre-shave tip is 82 mm3
  a side (two corners).

Profile
  Three tangent arcs, radii 30 / 40 / 30 mm, turn 15.5 / 31 / 15.5 deg. Belly |y|=45.95
  at D=50.7. Collar intrusion 0.95 mm. Inner face to the 69.2 mm tyre about 8.9 mm.
  OD 45 clearance 1.5 mm. Stanchion clearance 6.0 mm. The check string is unchanged from
  v2.19.1. Production file and test bundle feature bodies are the same text.
