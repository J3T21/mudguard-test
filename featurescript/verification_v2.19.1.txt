v2.19.1 verification (offline, no Onshape)

Why v2.19 failed
  Onshape threw BOOLEAN_BAD_INPUT at the intersection that trimmed the wing outline to the
  bowed slab (id wing + "ix"). That slab was a loft of 21 rectangles, and on the long flat
  run many of those sections were the same rectangle. The flat outer face also lay exactly
  on the |y|=48.5 wall plane. The production file still contained the v2.18 checks (indent
  at |y|=42.7, the R10 blend, and a hard fail on leg clearance), so it would have thrown
  after a successful build.

What the sheet is now
  One closed profile in the YZ plane, extruded once along X. No loft.
  Above the wall edge the outer face is 0.05 mm proud of |y|=48.5, so the union with the
  guard shares volume instead of two coincident faces. The clip that already limits the
  guard to |y|<=48.5 shaves that proud strip back flush.
  From the wall edge the profile is three circular arcs, each tangent to the next:
    radius 30 mm turning 15.5 deg, radius 40 mm turning back through 31 deg, radius 30 mm
    turning 15.5 deg onto the lower flat. The lower flat is |y|=48.5 down to the tip.
  The inner boundary is the same curves offset 2.5 mm. The two bottom corners are R8 bites,
  subtracted, each tool overlapping the sheet.

Offline checks on that profile (featurescript/v2_19_1_profile.py)
  Closed area 273.7 mm2. 120 vertices. Zero-length edges: 0. Self-intersections: 0.
  Offset distance 2.5 mm at every sample.
  The only corner that is not tangent is the 0.05 mm step where the proud embed meets the
  wall plane. The three arcs meet with continuous tangent, and the path returns to
  |y|=48.5 with a vertical tangent.
  Embed overlap with the wall, one side: 8.8 mm2. That is shared volume, not a shared face.
  Belly |y|=45.95 at 50.7 mm below the brace. Collar intrusion 0.95 mm.
  The root line is still 2.6 mm into the upper leg; that is the tangent on the wall plane.
  Max-tyre gap of the inner face: 8.9 mm. OD 45 clearance 1.5 mm. Stanchion clearance 6.0 mm.

The check
  featurescript/mudguard_v2.19.1.fs and featurescript/test_bundle_v2.19.1.fs throw the same
  string: v2.19.1 volA volB tyreGap stanchion collar od45 legMin holes bedB rootY bowY.
