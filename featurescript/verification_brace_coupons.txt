How to print / fit
------------------
1. Slice C0.stl first (PETG, P1S). Count the bars on the tyre side: 1=C0, 2=C1, 3=C2.
2. The cavity faces the brace. The narrow end is the seat-tube end and enters first.
3. If C0 will not start, try C1, then C2. The flares lap onto the stays.
4. The crown comes from the bridge in the cropped scan (scan X 135–158).
   If it rocks, that was not the contact face and the crown needs another pass.
