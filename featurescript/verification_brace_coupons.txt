Intent
------
The production C1 under-brace section is still a flat top (PH5=60, R18, W=82).
Photos of the Fox AWL brace show a continuous arch over the tyre, not a flat.
These four short coupons are print-fit probes for that arch before changing C1.
They are NOT the mudguard; they only seat in the brace cavity at tyre-top height.

How to print / fit
------------------
1. Slice any A*.stl in Bambu Studio (PETG, P1S). Flat floor on bed.
2. Seat the coupon under the brace at the tyre-top height with crown toward the brace.
3. Pick the rise that seats without rocking and without forcing the legs apart.
4. Only then paste brace_arch_coupons.fs (or bake the winning rise into C1).
