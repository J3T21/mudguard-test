How to print / fit
------------------
1. Slice C0.stl first (PETG, P1S). Floor down, crown up. Count the end ridges: 1=C0, 2=C1, 3=C2.
2. The narrow end is the seat-tube end. It has to enter first.
3. If C0 will not start, try C1, then C2.
4. The crown is asymmetric. If it rocks, the slice used for the crown
   (scan X ≈ −25 mm) is not the contact face, and the crown needs another pass.
