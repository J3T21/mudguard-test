Intent
------
Print-fit probes for an arched under-brace section before changing production C1.
Top corners use production-style R18 fillets with a crown arc tangent to both, so
wall → fillet → crown is G1 (no sharp lump at the shoulder).

Geometry (all mm)
-----------------
Outer width          88.0   (vertical walls at ±44; ~1 mm/side vs ~90 mm cavity)
Corner fillets       18.0   (centres at ±26, y=-18)
Wall thickness        2.5
Length (extrusion)   50.0
Depth (U opening)    30.0
Crown                circular through (0, rise), internally tangent to both R18 fillets
                     R = (hx² + (rise+18)² - 18²) / (2·rise),  hx = 26

Variant  rise   R_crown  ID nubs
A1         2.5  154.450  1
A2         4.0  104.500  2
A3         5.5   82.205  3
A4         7.0   69.786  4

Fix notes
---------
Earlier STLs had a broken crown polyline (arc traversed the wrong way, then
endpoints snapped), which put a sharp lump at the top corner. Regenerated with
left→right crown through the peak and vertical walls so wall–fillet is G1.

Offline checks (python featurescript/brace_arch_coupons.py)
-----------------------------------------------------------
A1..A4: peaks match rise; upper path x monotonic; wall–fillet / fillet–crown
junction turns < 3° (sampling). STLs regenerated.

How to print / fit
------------------
1. Slice any A*.stl in Bambu Studio (PETG, P1S). Flat floor on bed.
2. Seat under the brace at tyre-top height, crown up.
3. Pick the rise that seats without rocking.
4. Bake the winning rise into C1 (keep R18); optional paste brace_arch_coupons.fs.

Onshape economy
---------------
