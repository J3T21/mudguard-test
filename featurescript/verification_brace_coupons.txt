Intent
------
The production C1 under-brace section is still a flat top (PH5=60, R18, W=82).
Photos of the Fox AWL brace show a continuous arch over the tyre, not a flat.
These four short coupons are print-fit probes for that arch before changing C1.
They are NOT the mudguard; they only seat in the brace cavity at tyre-top height.
Top corners use the same R18 fillets as production C1 so a winning rise can drop
straight into the final under-brace profile.

Geometry (all mm)
-----------------
Outer width          88.0   (~1 mm clearance per side vs ~90 mm cavity)
Wall-to-wall         82.0   (matches production C1 width)
Corner fillets       18.0   (production C1; centres at ±23, y=-18)
Wall thickness        2.5
Length (extrusion)   50.0
Depth (U opening)    30.0
Crown shape          circular arc through (0, rise), internally tangent to both R18 fillets
                     R = (hx² + (rise+18)² - 18²) / (2·rise),  hx = 23

How to print / fit
------------------
1. Slice any A*.stl in Bambu Studio (PETG, P1S). Flat floor on bed.
2. Seat the coupon under the brace at the tyre-top height with crown toward the brace.
3. Pick the rise that seats without rocking and without forcing the legs apart.
4. Only then paste brace_arch_coupons.fs (or bake the winning rise into C1 with R18 kept).
